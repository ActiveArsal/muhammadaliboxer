About Me

I am Muhammad Ali, a professional British Boxer. I made boxing history on 9th May 2018 after becoming the UK's first diabetic to be granted a professional licence by the British Boxing Board of Control (BBBOfC). I am an ambassador for Diabetes UK and on track to be world champion in the near future.

“Diabetes is a condition, not an illness; I’m just like any other ordinary person. Life is not about waiting for the storm to pass by but learning to weather the storm. One day I’d love to say I lived by diabetes”
